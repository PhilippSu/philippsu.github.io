01 / About

Engineering across the physical and digital system.

My path began in mechanical engineering and later expanded into mechatronics, robotics, and medical technology. This foundation shapes how I approach robots: as complete physical systems, not isolated software components.

I combine hands-on hardware development—design, fabrication, assembly, and iteration—with multimodal sensing and robotic system integration. My current work spans commercial embodied robot deployment and research on instrumented ultrasound systems for medical robotics.

01

CAMP, TUMMaster Thesis / Medical RoboticsOngoing

Instrumented Freehand Ultrasound for Robot Learning

Designed and iteratively optimized an instrumented ultrasound device integrating an ultrasound probe, Intel RealSense RGB-D camera, NDI tracking marker, and six-axis force/torque sensor for freehand human demonstrations and later robot mounting.

The ongoing work includes multimodal ROS 2 data acquisition, extrinsic calibration, coordinate transformations, pose-dependent gravity compensation, temporal alignment, and preparation of robot-learning demonstrations. Learning baselines and robot evaluation are planned next steps.

  • Medical Robotics
  • ROS 2
  • Multimodal Sensing
  • RGB-D
  • NDI Tracking
  • 6D Force/Torque
  • Calibration
  • LeRobot

02

MIMED, TUMSemester Thesis / Research

Adaptive Compliant Robotic Gripper

Designed and developed an adaptive robotic gripper with one-shot FDM-printed rigid–flexible hybrid fingers, including mechanical design, compliant mechanism development, prototyping, and experimental evaluation.

The work later resulted in a peer-reviewed article in Mechatronics.
